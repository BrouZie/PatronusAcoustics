# Design Validation

This document summarises simulation-based design validation for the dual-ring acoustic drone detection system. All metrics are generated by parameter sweeps over the `config/sweep/` YAML files.

> **Staleness caveat**: these sweeps predate the mid-2026 physics refactor (corrected ISO 9613-1 absorption, sensor imperfections enabled in `default.yaml`, sensor-chain rework, front/back metrics). The qualitative findings and rankings hold; absolute detection rates — particularly the long-range tables — should be re-run before being quoted in a design decision. For geometry selection use `make compare-baseline` ([analysis.md](analysis.md)), which always runs on current physics.

## Mounting Height & Ground Reflection

Mounting height determines the interference pattern from ground-reflected multipath. At low heights, the direct and reflected paths interfere destructively at certain elevation angles, creating detection nulls.

### Sweep Design

`config/sweep/mounting_height.yaml` varies:

- **Height**: 0.3, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0 m
- **Ground model**: constant (R=0.8) vs Delany-Bazley (σ=200 kPa·s/m², grass)
- **Duration**: 5.0 s, oscillating trajectory (7–37 m range)

### Results

| Height (m) | Model         | Det Rate | Ang Error | Mean PSR | Beamwidth |
|------------|---------------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 0.3        | constant      | 49%      | 2.5°      | 0.43 dB  | 38.9°     |
| 0.3        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.32 dB  | 106.9°    |
| 0.5        | constant      | 48%      | 2.5°      | 0.39 dB  | 38.4°     |
| 0.5        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.27 dB  | 108.4°    |
| 0.75       | constant      | 48%      | 2.3°      | 0.40 dB  | 40.2°     |
| 0.75       | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.25 dB  | 109.1°    |
| 1.0        | constant      | 48%      | 2.4°      | 0.41 dB  | 39.9°     |
| 1.0        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.25 dB  | 109.0°    |
| 1.5        | constant      | 52%      | 2.7°      | 0.44 dB  | 36.3°     |
| 1.5        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.24 dB  | 110.1°    |
| 2.0        | constant      | 48%      | 2.4°      | 0.41 dB  | 40.3°     |
| 2.0        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.20 dB  | 110.0°    |
| 3.0        | constant      | 52%      | 2.7°      | 0.43 dB  | 33.8°     |
| 3.0        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.22 dB  | 111.0°    |
| 5.0        | constant      | 52%      | 2.7°      | 0.43 dB  | 36.0°     |
| 5.0        | delany_bazley | 0%       | —         | 0.24 dB  | 115.1°    |

**Key finding**: Mounting height has minimal impact on detection rate with the constant ground model (47–52% across all heights). Height primarily affects the angular error distribution and beamwidth, not the detection rate, at this SNR range.

**Ground model sensitivity**: The Delany-Bazley grass model kills detection entirely (0% at all heights). This is physical behaviour — grass absorption (|R| ~ 0.6–0.8 at mid frequencies, complex phase) smears the reflection into an incoherent background, destroying the beamforming coherence gain that the constant model preserves. The constant model is a best-case approximation that should not be relied upon for absolute performance predictions.

**Impact on practical design**: Since Delany-Bazley is physically accurate for grass/mixed terrain, the 0% detection rate across all heights means the practical system cannot rely on ground-reflection coherence at this SNR. The system should be designed assuming the ground is purely absorptive, and all detection will come from the direct path alone. Height selection should be driven by other factors (wind noise shielding, physical mounting constraints, FOV coverage).

### Recommended Height

**1.5–3.0 m** is the practical range. At constant ground model, detection is flat (48–52%) across all heights, so the choice is driven by:
- Wind noise: lower heights (< 1 m) experience higher turbulence near the ground
- Physical obstructions: 1.5 m clears typical undergrowth, 3 m clears vehicle roofs
- Deployment convenience: tripod at 1.5 m is single-person deployable; 3+ m requires two people

The default of 5.0 m is unnecessarily high but produces equivalent results.

## Frequency Band Analysis

The detection bandwidth is set by `min_freq` and `max_freq` in the SRP-PHAT config.

### Sweep Design

`config/sweep/freq_band.yaml` varies:

- **min_freq**: 0, 300, 500, 700 Hz
- **max_freq**: 1500, 2000, 3000 Hz
- **Duration**: 5.0 s, oscillating trajectory (7–37 m range)

### Results

| min_freq | max_freq | Det Rate | Ang Error | Mean PSR | Beamwidth |
|----------|----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 0        | 1500     | 82%      | 6.2°      | 0.26 dB  | 34.2°     |
| 0        | 2000     | 89%      | 5.5°      | 0.36 dB  | 29.8°     |
| 0        | 3000     | 64%      | 3.8°      | 0.44 dB  | 30.4°     |
| **300**  | **1500** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.33 dB  | 28.0°     |
| **300**  | **2000** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.44 dB  | 24.6°     |
| **300**  | **3000** | **99.8%**| 6.1°      | 0.52 dB  | 23.8°     |
| **500**  | **1500** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.39 dB  | 24.5°     |
| **500**  | **2000** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.53 dB  | 21.7°     |
| **500**  | **3000** | **99.8%**| 6.1°      | 0.61 dB  | 20.9°     |
| **700**  | **1500** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.48 dB  | 22.0°     |
| **700**  | **2000** | **100%** | 6.1°      | 0.61 dB  | 19.6°     |
| 700      | 3000     | 91%      | 5.7°      | 0.67 dB  | 20.2°     |

**Critical insight**: Including frequencies below 300 Hz (min_freq=0) **degrades** detection from 100% to 64–89%. The full band (0–3000 Hz) performs the worst at 64% — the low-frequency wind/environmental noise and the overly-narrow high-frequency beam both hurt PSR.

**Optimal band**: 300–2000 Hz — 100% detection, 6.1° angular error. This band:
1. Rejects sub-300 Hz wind noise (dominant noise source)
2. Captures harmonics 2–10 of the ~200 Hz blade-pass frequency
3. Avoids spatial aliasing (inner-ring alias-free up to 1320 Hz; outer ring D/2λ < 1 at 2000 Hz)
4. Keeps atmospheric absorption well under 0.5 dB at 37 m max range

min_freq=500 gives identical performance to min_freq=300, confirming that the 300–500 Hz band adds no useful information in this scenario.

### Recommendation

**min_freq = 500 Hz, max_freq = 2000 Hz**. This band:
1. Rejects all wind noise (energy concentrated below 200 Hz)
2. Captures BPF harmonics 3–10 — sufficient spectral content for PHAT weighting
3. Beamwidth of 21.7° gives a good balance between angular resolution and robustness
4. Provides margin against spatial aliasing (inner ring D/λ = 0.56 at 2 kHz)

## Detection Range

The maximum detection range depends on drone SPL, ambient noise, and the beamforming array gain.

### Sweep Design

`config/sweep/detection_range.yaml` varies:

- **Distance**: 10, 20, 30, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 300, 500 m
- **Duration**: 5.0 s, oscillating trajectory, full realism (birds, wind, turbulence, ground at 1.5 m)
- **Default band**: 0–3000 Hz (full band — suboptimal)

### Practical Detection Limit

With the default full-band (0–3000 Hz) configuration, detection drops to 0% at 100 m. The effective SNR falls from 24 dB at 10 m to 3.6 dB at 100 m.

With the **optimal band (500–2000 Hz)** the practical limit extends dramatically:

| Distance | Det Rate (Full Band) | Det Rate (Optimal Band) |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| 10 m     | 65%                  | **100%**                |
| 20 m     | 59%                  | **100%**                |
| 30 m     | 53%                  | **100%**                |
| 50 m     | 30%                  | **100%**                |
| 75 m     | 1.3%                 | **100%**                |
| 100 m    | 0%                   | **92%**                 |
| 150 m    | 0%                   | **64%**                 |
| 200 m    | 0%                   | **43%**                 |
| 300 m    | 0%                   | **7%**                  |
| 500 m    | 0%                   | **0%**                  |

The optimal band (500–2000 Hz) provides **100% detection out to 75 m** and useful detection (43–64%) at 150–200 m. The improvement comes from rejecting sub-500 Hz environmental noise (wind, traffic rumble) which otherwise dominates the cross-spectral density matrix and flattens the SRP-PHAT map.

### Practical Recommendations

| Target Range | Configuration |
|---|---|
| ≤ 75 m | Full band (0–3000 Hz) is adequate; 500–2000 Hz recommended for robustness |
| 100–150 m | **Must use** 500–2000 Hz band; 16-mic array sufficient |
| 150–200 m | 500–2000 Hz band + 32-mic array (3 dB gain) recommended |
| > 200 m | Requires larger drone (80+ dB at 1 m) or very quiet environment |

### Range Extension Options

1. **Optimised frequency band** (500–2000 Hz): Demonstrated range improvement from 50 m → 150 m (3×)
2. **Lower ambient noise**: Each 3 dB reduction extends range by ~20% (quieter rural environment)
3. **Higher drone SPL**: Larger drones (73 dB → 80 dB at 1 m) extend range by factor ~2.2×
4. **Array gain**: 16 → 32 mics adds 3 dB (10 log₁₀(32/16))

## Gate Threshold Tuning

Detection uses two cascaded gates:

### Gate 1 (Energy + Tonality)

Configuration: `compute_gate1(rms_threshold_db, flatness_threshold)`

- **rms_threshold_db**: RMS of band-limited (500–4000 Hz) signal must exceed the ambient floor (5th percentile over all frames) by this amount
- **flatness_threshold**: Spectral flatness must be below this value (drone harmonics are strongly tonal, flatness ≈ 0.03–0.1)

Default: `rms_threshold_db = 5.0`, `flatness_threshold = 0.5`

Effects of varying rms_threshold_db:
- 3 dB: Gate 1 fires on ~20% of frames (very sensitive, may include noise transients)
- 5 dB: Fires on ~14% of frames (recommended — catches closest approaches)
- 8 dB: Fires on 0% of frames (too conservative for the available SNR)

### Gate 2 (SRP-PHAT PSR)

Configuration: `srpphat.detection.psr_threshold_db`

### Results

| Threshold | 20 m Det | 20 m Error | 50 m Det | 50 m Error | 100 m |
|-----------|----------|------------|----------|------------|-------|
| 2 dB      | 63%      | 3.6°       | 25%      | 1.5°       | 0.2%  |
| 3 dB      | 62%      | 3.5°       | 29%      | 1.7°       | 0%    |
| 4 dB      | 58%      | 3.2°       | 27%      | 1.4°       | 0%    |
| 6 dB      | 59%      | 3.3°       | 28%      | 1.6°       | 0%    |
| 8 dB      | 61%      | 3.5°       | 25%      | 1.6°       | 0.2%  |
| 10 dB     | 61%      | 3.4°       | 27%      | 1.6°       | 0.2%  |

**Key finding**: PSR threshold from 2–10 dB has negligible effect on detection rate. At 20 m, all thresholds give 58–63%; at 50 m, 25–29%. This means the PSR distribution is bimodal — either well above 10 dB (confident detection) or well below 2 dB (noise-dominated). The threshold is not the limiting factor in this scenario.

### Recommended Threshold

**6 dB** — no worse than any other value, and standard practice for outlier rejection. The 2 dB threshold's single false-positive frame at 100 m (0.2%) suggests that lower thresholds are not useful for extending range.

## Frame Rejection (One-Sided Windscreen)

The `wind_coherence_directionality` metric provides a baseline for the one-sided windscreen design. Without shielding, forward and rear mics show identical adjacent-pair coherence (symmetric). At the compact array scale (0.13–0.26 m mic spacing), wind noise coherence is negligible above 100 Hz even without a windscreen.

A practical windscreen would reduce the wind-induced pressure at the rear mics by ~6–10 dB (acoustic foam + resistive fabric). The simulation's `wind_coherence_directionality` output would then show lower rear-mic coherence with the forward set, directly validating the windscreen's effect.

### Baseline Coherence (1.5 m/s wind, no windscreen)

| Frequency | Forward Adjacent | Rear Adjacent | Fwd–Rev Cross |
|-----------|------------------|---------------|---------------|
| 200 Hz    | 1.04 × 10⁻⁴     | 1.04 × 10⁻⁴   | 2.91 × 10⁻⁷  |
| 500 Hz    | 3.41 × 10⁻⁶     | 3.41 × 10⁻⁶   | 5.61 × 10⁻¹⁰ |

Wind coherence is symmetric (forward = rear) and negligible above 200 Hz, confirming that the compact 0.13–0.26 m spacing decorrelates wind pressure fluctuations effectively. A windscreen's benefit at these frequencies would be from reducing the absolute wind pressure level, not from altering the coherence structure.

## Simulation Assumptions

1. **Free-field propagation**: No walls, buildings, or terrain beyond the flat ground plane
2. **Stationary source model**: Drone is a point source at the given SPL; no explicit Doppler correction beyond per-sample trajectory delays
3. **AWGN noise model**: Sensor noise is white Gaussian at the EIN budget; environmental noise sources have spectral shaping but are stationary
4. **Array calibration**: These historical sweeps ran with a perfectly matched array. Current `default.yaml` enables realistic ICS-52000 imperfections (`mic.imperfections`), which re-runs will include
5. **Bandwidth effect**: The detection_range sweep with full band (0–3000 Hz) gives a pessimistic ~50 m practical limit; the 500–2000 Hz band extends this to ~150 m. Always use the optimal band for realistic range predictions.
